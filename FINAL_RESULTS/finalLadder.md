### Final score computation

The final score is computed with the following weights:

- $k_{batt} = 10$
- $k_{tau} = 2$
- $k_{time} = 0.5$
- $k_{e} = 0.1$
- $\mathbf{k}_{cost} = [0.15\,0.1\,0.3\,0.8]$

Using:

- $\phi_{time} = \mathrm{SimTime}$
- $\phi_{cost} = [\phi_{cost,fast},\,\phi_{cost,slow},\,\phi_{cost,wpt},\,\phi_{cost,emerg}]^T$
- $\phi_E = E_{BP} + \mathbf{1}^T\mathbf{\phi}_{cost}$

the final score is:

$$
\mathrm{Final Score} =
k_{batt}\,\phi_{batt} +
k_{tau}\,\phi_{tau} +
k_{time}\,\phi_{time} +
k_e\,\phi_E +
\mathbf{k}_{cost}^T\mathbf{\phi}_{cost}.
$$
